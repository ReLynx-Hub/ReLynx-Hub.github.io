Privacy Policy

What ReLynx stores, why, and what you can do about it. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Who’s responsible for your data

ReLynx, run by an independent developer in Northern Ireland, UK, is the data controller. Contact us through Patreon messages or in our Discord server.

We don’t store the text of ordinary chat messages. Server logs and ticket transcripts are posted into channels in your own server, not kept by us. We never see or store card details; Patreon handles payments under its own privacy policy. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it’s kept

ReLynx runs on a server rented from OVHcloud in Canada. The UK recognises Canada as giving adequate protection for personal data. The server only allows key-based logins and is backed up daily.

How long we keep it

  • Server data stays while ReLynx is in your server. If ReLynx is removed, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Billing records stay for as long as the law requires for tax, usually 6 years.
  • Daily backups are kept for 7 days, then deleted.
  • You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see below). Server owners can also download everything ReLynx stores for their server with /relynx export.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Contact us through Patreon messages and we’ll reply within one month. Server owners can remove ReLynx at any time to stop new data being collected.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13. We don’t ask members for their age, and we only collect the details listed above.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll post it in our Discord server before it applies.
